Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a service appointment?

Fill out the appointment form on this page and select “Send Request on WhatsApp”. Review the message in WhatsApp and send it to our team. Your appointment is confirmed only after the team replies.

Can I enquire about a vehicle with a driver?

Yes. Select “Vehicle rental with driver” in the form and include your travel date, route, number of passengers and other requirements.

Are rental vehicles available for self-drive?

Yes, self-drive rentals are available. Select "Self-Drive" in the enquiry form to discuss vehicle options, availability and pricing with our team.

Are the service prices listed online?

Please contact the team with your vehicle details and requested service for current pricing and availability.
